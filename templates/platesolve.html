How plate solving works
1. The target name is resolved to RA/Dec coordinates via SIMBAD.
2. An image is captured live or uploaded from disk.
3. Stars are detected in the image.
4. Reference stars are queried from the local HYG v4.1 catalogue (83K stars, mag < 9).
5. Triangle pattern matching derives the WCS (World Coordinate System).
6. NGC / IC / Messier objects in the field are identified.
7. A Siril-style annotated image is rendered.
